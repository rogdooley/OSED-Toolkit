OSED WinDbg Commands - easy Profile (VM-Only)
=============================================

Target profile
- easy: /GS- /DYNAMICBASE:NO /NXCOMPAT:NO

Launch
- windbg -g -G osed_vulnsvc.exe 9999

Setup
- .symfix
- .reload /f
- sxe av

Focus
- Recover the protocol first, then obtain EIP control through the simplest
  vulnerable handler.

Commands
- !analyze -v
- r
- kb
- dd esp L60
- u eip-20 L40

Offset workflow
- Use the client you wrote from your protocol-reversing notes to send a cyclic
  pattern to the identified overflow handler.
- On crash:
  - r eip
  - dd esp L100

Badchar workflow
- Send a bad-character payload with your recovered protocol client.
- Inspect:
  - db esp L100
  - db <buffer_address> L100

Redirect workflow
- Inventory both osed_vulnsvc.exe and osedhelper.dll.
- Locate and verify a direct stack-transfer instruction in the supplied modules.
- Confirm the selected module has ASLR disabled before using its absolute address.

Notes
- Keep payloads benign and local VM only.
